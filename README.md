# Executive Sales Analysis (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end **Executive Sales Performance Analysis** built using Microsoft Excel. This project evaluates key revenue drivers, regional sales trends, product category performance, and target achievements to provide leadership with actionable, data-backed business strategy recommendations.

---

## 📁 Deliverables Included
* **`executive_sales_report.docx`** — Detailed executive report covering business strategy, regional breakdown, and performance metrics.
* **`executive_sales_presentation.pptx`** — Executive slide deck designed for stakeholder updates and strategic reviews.
* **`sales_dashboard.png`** — Visual preview of the main executive sales dashboard.
* 🔗 **[Download Full Interactive Excel Workbook (Google Drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1zaC89W86Pjm_9LvaOTxezyJ_JGLOAFl3?usp=sharing)** — Complete interactive workbook featuring dynamic Pivot Tables, slicers, formulas, and full raw dataset.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Excel Concepts Used
* **Microsoft Excel**: Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts, Slicers & Timelines, VLOOKUP / XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, Dynamic Data Modeling, Conditional Formatting.
* **Microsoft Word**: Business Reporting, Strategy Recommendations, & Performance Summaries.
* **Microsoft PowerPoint**: Data Storytelling & Executive Presentation Design.

## 💡 Key Insights & Executive Summary
* **Revenue Leaders:** Identified top-performing product categories and core sales regions driving the majority of total revenue growth.
* **Performance Gaps:** Pinpointed underperforming channels and quarterly revenue drops to target for operational improvement.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Highlighted data-driven opportunities to optimize pricing, shift regional sales focus, and increase profit margins.
